Winter Cardio Contingency

  • Outdoor running stays default as long as conditions allow — proper gear extends the season further than expected
  • Non-treadmill indoor backup: assault bike, rower, ski erg, stair repeats — rotate to fight boredom
  • Check campus/community rec for an indoor track or field house before defaulting to treadmill
  • Bronco/broken-Bronco work doesn't need to pause for weather
  • Pair winter sessions with a teammate/friend — structural fix, not a willpower ask
⚠ Speed work, max-effort sprints, and lower-body plyometrics are gated by PT clearance (quad symmetry, hop tests) — not added independently, given ACL history.
